How to use Open Planning Poker

A tour of the game from the first click to the PDF report: the room, the roles, voting and the reveal, the final estimate, and the features that help a team estimate faster. Nothing needs an account; everyone joins with a link.

1. Create a room

Open the app and choose Create game. Give the room a name, for example "Sprint 42 refinement", and pick a deck: Fibonacci, Fibonacci v2, T-shirt sizes, powers of 2, or your own values. The preview shows the cards before you start. A description is optional. If you are not sure which deck to use, read cards and scales.

2. Invite the team

Choose Invite to show the link and a QR code. People in the room scan the code with their phone; remote people open the link. Everyone gets a random name, which they can change by clicking it in the header. Names are deleted after 24 hours.

3. Add the tickets

Add tickets one by one with New ticket, or use Import to paste a list (one ticket per line) or upload a CSV or JSON file from your tracker. Descriptions support a little Markdown: bold, italic, code, lists and links. You can edit, delete and drag tickets into order from the ticket list.

4. Roles: host, co-hosts, players and observers

  • Host: the person who created the room. Only the host can delete it or hand it over to someone else.
  • Co-hosts: players the host trusts to run the session. They add tickets, reveal the cards, start the timer and change the room options.
  • Players: everyone who votes.
  • Observers: people who only watch, such as a stakeholder or a new colleague. They have no cards and are not counted in "3/5 voted".

The host and co-hosts can remove a player; that player's votes stay on the tickets.

5. Vote in secret

Pick a card from your hand. Your card lands face down on the table, and the ring in the middle shows how many people have voted, never what they voted. On a keyboard you can type a card's value ("5", "13", "?") or move between cards with the arrow keys and press Enter.

The host can start a shared timer (30 seconds to 5 minutes). Everyone sees the same countdown, and the cards are revealed when it runs out. With auto-reveal on, the cards turn over as soon as every player has voted.

6. Reveal and talk

When the cards are revealed, the results show the distribution of votes, the average and the median, and how close the team is: consensus, close to consensus, or a wide spread. With a wide spread, the lowest and highest cards are marked and the results suggest who should explain first. Talk, then choose Vote again to start a clean round. The reasons for this are in what planning poker is.

7. Agree on the final estimate

After the reveal the host picks the card the team agreed on; the most common card is marked to make it quick. The final estimate is shown in the ticket list and used in the reports and the team's velocity. Then move to the next ticket.

8. Chat, reports and exports

  • Chat next to the table, with clickable links. It keeps the last 100 messages and deletes them after 24 hours.
  • PDF report of the whole session: every ticket with its votes, the estimate, the average, the median and how far apart the votes were.
  • CSV export of the results, to paste back into your tracker.

9. Estimate before the meeting

With async voting the host opens a voting window of 24 hours, 3 days or a week. Everyone votes on all the tickets when it suits them, and the cards stay hidden until the host reveals each ticket in the meeting.

10. Team rooms and velocity

A team room keeps one place for a team: an invite link, the members, every sprint planning session and a velocity chart with the points of each sprint and the average of the last three. Create one from Team rooms in the menu, then start each sprint planning from there.

Good to know

  • The app is in English, Serbian and German. It follows your browser, and you can change it in the menu.
  • Light and dark mode follow your system; switch in the menu or the footer.
  • Install the app on your phone or computer from the menu; it opens even without a connection.
  • Screen readers announce who voted, the reveal and the next ticket.

Frequently asked questions

Can more than one person run the room?

Yes. The host can make players co-hosts. Co-hosts add tickets, reveal the cards, run the timer and change the room options. The host can also hand the room over to someone else.

What happens to the votes when someone leaves?

Their votes stay on the tickets, so the results and the report do not change.

Can I join only to watch?

Yes. Switch to observer in the menu next to your name. Observers have no cards and are not counted in the number of votes.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Your cards stay at the bottom of the screen and the table shows everyone. You can also install the app on your phone from the menu.
