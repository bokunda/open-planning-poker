Support

Open Planning Poker is free and open source. Help comes from the guides, the community and the maintainer on GitHub.

Start here

  • How to use Open Planning Poker: rooms, roles, voting, the reveal and reports.
  • Integrations: Slack, Microsoft Teams and Jira.
  • Changelog: what changed in each release.

Common questions

  • The /poker command does not answer. Reinstall the app from the integrations page; the install may have been removed by a workspace admin.
  • The Teams tab is blank. Ask your Teams admin to allow the app, and check that app.openplanningpoker.com is not blocked by your network.
  • Save to Jira is missing. The ticket has to be imported from Jira, and the issue needs a Story Points field on its edit screen.

Report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub with what you did, what you expected and what happened. Do not post tokens or private data.

Add the badge

Using Open Planning Poker in your team? Put the badge in a README or team wiki. There is a light and a dark version; the dark one is badge-dark.svg.
