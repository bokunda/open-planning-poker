Story point estimation for Scrum beginners

Story points measure how big a piece of work is compared with other work, not how many hours it takes. Planning poker is the simplest way for a new team to agree on them.

Your first session in five steps

  1. Pick a reference ticket the whole team knows and call it a 3.
  2. Read the next ticket out loud; answer questions about what it means, not how long it takes.
  3. Everyone votes in secret, comparing it with the reference: smaller, about the same, or bigger?
  4. Reveal together. If the votes are far apart, the lowest and highest voters say why.
  5. Vote again once and take the card most people agree on. Move on.

Mistakes new teams make

  • Turning points back into hours. Points only work as relative size.
  • Discussing every ticket for ten minutes. Use a timer; a close vote is good enough.
  • Comparing velocity between teams. Every team's scale is its own.
