Build your own apps on the Open Planning Poker API

The public API and webhooks of Open Planning Poker are free: build a dashboard, connect a tool we do not support, or practise integrations against a real API with real data.

Sprint retros in the team room, starting from the numbers

Run the sprint retro where you planned the sprint: write in silence, group, vote with dots and carry action items over, with the sprint's estimation numbers on the board.

Share how a planning session went, in one link

A read-only recap link for stakeholders: tickets, final estimates, total points, consensus and discussion time, with a preview card in Slack, Teams and email.

AI help in planning poker that never picks a card

An optional assistant checks a ticket for gaps before voting, suggests how to split a big one, and summarizes the discussion in one sentence. It never suggests an estimate.

Organizations: every team room of a company in one place

Put the team rooms of a company under one organization: invite with a link, roles admin and member, and every team's first-vote agreement, re-votes and velocity side by side.

Run a better discussion after the reveal

Notes from the lowest and highest voters, a discussion timer, a confidence check, reactions and a needs-information flag: small tools that keep the talk after a split vote short and useful.
