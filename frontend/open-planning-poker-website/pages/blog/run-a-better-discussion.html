Run a better discussion after the reveal

The reveal is where planning poker pays off or wastes time. When the votes are far apart, the next five minutes decide whether the team learns something or just argues.

Let the outliers speak first

With outlier notes on, the lowest and highest voters can add a one-line reason with their vote. After the reveal everyone sees why someone voted 3 and someone 13, before anybody talks.

Keep it short

A discussion timer of two, three or five minutes starts after the reveal. When it ends, the team votes again or the host picks the estimate.

Know how sure the team is

A confidence check asks everyone, after the estimate, how sure they are from 1 to 5. Low confidence is worth a note in the ticket, and it shows up in the sprint retro.

Park what cannot be estimated

Needs more information marks a ticket that should go back to its author instead of being guessed. Reactions let people agree without interrupting.

All of it is in the room menu under the discussion options, and each part can be turned off.
