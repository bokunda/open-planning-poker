Organizations: every team room of a company in one place

When several teams estimate with Open Planning Poker, someone wants to see them together. Organizations do that without taking the rooms away from their teams.

How it works

  1. Create an organization from the app menu and share its invite link.
  2. Team owners move their own rooms in. They stay the owners; nothing else about the room changes.
  3. Admins see the organization's teams side by side and can download the table as CSV.

Numbers that help, not a ranking

The comparison shows first-vote agreement, re-votes per ticket and the velocity trend of each team over its last six sessions. Team numbers only, never per player.

Velocity is not comparable across teams, because every team has its own scale. The table is for spotting a team that suddenly re-votes a lot or whose velocity drops, so someone can ask what changed.

Roles

  • Admin: renames the organization, makes a new invite link, changes roles, removes members and teams, and sees the numbers.
  • Member: sees the organization's teams and can move their own team in or out.
