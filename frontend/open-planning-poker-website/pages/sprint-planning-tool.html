Free sprint planning tool: capacity, budget and the plan

A sprint plan is a guess about the future. It gets better when it starts from how much the team really finished and who is around this time.

From estimates to a plan

  1. Estimate the candidate tickets with planning poker or the sorting board.
  2. Set the capacity: the sprint length and each member's available days, in half days.
  3. Get a budget from the last sprints: points per person-day when the days are known, otherwise the average.
  4. Pick the tickets that fit; a bar shows how full the sprint is. Copy the plan as text, or move the Jira issues into a sprint.

Try the formula without an account in the velocity calculator.

After the sprint

Run the retro in the same team room: write in silence, group, vote with dots and agree on action items, with the sprint's numbers on the board. Open action items come back at the next retro.
