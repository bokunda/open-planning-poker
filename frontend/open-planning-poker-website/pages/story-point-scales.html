Which scale to choose

  • Fibonacci is the usual default: the gaps grow with the size, so nobody argues about 8 versus 9.
  • Modified Fibonacci rounds the big values to 20, 40 and 100: good for refinement, when some items are still epics.
  • Powers of 2 doubles at every step; easy to reason about, fewer options in the middle.
  • T-shirt sizes keep the talk about relative size. Use the converter when you later need numbers for velocity.

The example tickets are only a starting point. The best reference is your own team's past work: pin two or three finished tickets per card in a team room and show them while voting.

More background on the special cards (? and the break card) is in the guide to planning poker cards.

Questions

Why do story point scales have gaps?

Large items cannot be estimated precisely. Gaps that grow with the size make the team choose between clearly different sizes instead of arguing about small differences.

How do I turn T-shirt sizes into points?

Agree on one point value per size with your team, for example S = 2, M = 3, L = 5, and keep it the same across sprints so velocity stays comparable.
