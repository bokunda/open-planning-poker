How the suggestion is made

When at least two past sprints have person-days (the sum of the days every member was available), the calculator divides the points by the person-days of each sprint. The suggestion is that rate times the person-days of the next sprint. The range uses the slowest and the fastest of those sprints.

Without person-days it takes the average of the last three sprints, and the range is their lowest and highest result. Both use the last three sprints only, because older sprints say less about the team as it is now.

Use it as a guide, not a target

Velocity describes the past. A new member, a holiday or a sprint full of unknown work changes it. Plan a little under the suggestion when the work is new, and talk about the range rather than the single number.

Let the app keep the numbers

Questions

What is sprint velocity?

Velocity is the number of story points a team finishes in a sprint. The average of the last few sprints is a good guess for the next one.

What are person-days?

The sum of the working days each member is available in the sprint. Four people for ten days, with one person away for two, is 38 person-days.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and sends nothing to a server.
