Async planning poker: estimate before the meeting

In async planning poker everyone votes on the tickets before the meeting, each person when it suits them. The votes stay hidden until the meeting, where the team reveals them together and talks only about the tickets where they differ.

When it helps

  • Teams across time zones, where a long live session means someone joins at night.
  • Long backlogs: twenty tickets live take an hour; voted in advance, the meeting covers only the four or five where people disagree.
  • People who think better on their own, with time to read the ticket and look at the code before they pick a card.

It helps less for brand-new work that nobody understands yet. Those tickets need a live conversation first, so leave them for the meeting.

How it works in Open Planning Poker

  1. Prepare the tickets

    Add the tickets to a room, or import them from a CSV or JSON export. Write a short description for each, because people will read it without you there to explain.

  2. Open the voting window

    As the host, open async voting and choose how long it stays open: 24 hours, 3 days or a week. Share the room link in the team chat.

  3. Everyone votes on their own

    Each player goes through the tickets and picks a card for each. A counter shows how many of the tickets you have voted on; nobody sees the values. You can change a vote until the ticket is revealed.

  4. Reveal in the meeting

    In the meeting the host reveals one ticket at a time. Where the votes agree, pick the final estimate and move on. Where they differ, the people with the lowest and highest cards explain, and the team votes again live.

Tips

  • Set a deadline a few hours before the meeting, so there is time to remind the people who have not voted.
  • Use the "?" card for tickets that are unclear. Several "?" votes tell the product owner which tickets need more detail before the meeting.
  • Do not discuss estimates in the chat before the reveal; that brings back the anchoring the secret vote removes.
  • Keep a team room for the team, so each sprint planning and its velocity stay in one place. See how to use Open Planning Poker.

Async or live?

LiveAsync
Best forNew or unclear work, small backlogsLong backlogs, distributed teams
Meeting timeEvery ticket is discussedOnly the tickets where votes differ
DiscussionRight after each voteIn the meeting, after the reveal

Many teams mix the two: async for the routine tickets, live for the big ones. The rules of a round are the same either way; see what planning poker is and how to run a session.

Frequently asked questions

Can people see each other's votes before the meeting?

No. Votes stay hidden on the server until the host reveals each ticket, so nobody is influenced by the first number they see.

Does async voting replace the meeting?

No. It makes the meeting shorter. Tickets where everyone agrees take seconds; the meeting is spent on the tickets where the votes differ.

Can someone change their vote?

Yes. Until the host reveals a ticket, players can pick a different card.
