Integrations: Slack, Microsoft Teams and Jira

Open Planning Poker works where your team already works. Every integration is free and needs no account: players still join with a link.

Slack

Type /poker Sprint 43 in any channel. Open Planning Poker creates a room and posts a Join button. Only you get the link to run the game. After each reveal, the ticket and its estimate are posted in the thread.

Add to Slack

Microsoft Teams

Add Open Planning Poker as a tab in a meeting, a channel or a chat. Start a new game or show one you already have. Everyone votes from the tab, in the meeting side panel or on the shared stage.

A Teams admin uploads the app package once in the Teams admin center. The package open-planning-poker-teams.zip is attached to every release on GitHub.

Jira Cloud

Connect your Jira site once from the import dialog. From Jira imports the issues of a sprint, a board or a JQL query, with their story points. After the reveal, Save to KEY writes the agreed estimate to the issue, optionally with a comment showing the votes and the notes of the lowest and highest voters.

What is stored

  • Slack and Jira tokens are stored encrypted on the server and never reach the browser. Disconnecting Jira deletes its tokens.
  • Teams needs no tokens: the tab is the normal web app inside Teams.
  • Read the privacy policy for the details.
