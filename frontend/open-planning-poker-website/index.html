How it works

One round takes a minute.

  1. 1

    Create a room

    Name it, pick a deck (Fibonacci, T-shirt sizes, powers of 2 or your own) and add tickets, or import them from CSV or JSON.

  2. 2

    Invite the team

    Share the link or let people scan the QR code. Everyone joins with a random name they can change. No accounts.

  3. 3

    Vote, then reveal

    Cards stay face down until the host reveals them. The results show the average, the median and who should explain.

Hidden voting

Everyone picks a card in secret.

You see who has voted, not what they voted. The ring in the middle fills as cards land on the table, and the host can start a timer to keep the pace.

  • Votes stay hidden on the server until the reveal
  • Type a card's value to vote: "5", "13", "?"
  • Chat next to the table, links are clickable

The reveal

Cards flip together. The outliers talk first.

When the votes are far apart, the lowest and highest cards are marked and the results suggest who should explain. Vote again in one click, or move to the next ticket.

  • Average, median and the spread of votes
  • Every ticket keeps its votes and estimate
  • PDF report of the whole session

Your deck

The cards your team already uses.

Fibonacci, modified Fibonacci, T-shirt sizes and powers of 2 are one click away, with a preview of the cards. Or type your own values.

Frequently asked questions

What is Open Planning Poker?

A free, open-source tool for estimating work together. The team votes on each ticket in secret, the cards are revealed at the same moment, and the team talks about the differences before settling on an estimate.

How does it work?

The host creates a room, picks a deck and shares the link or QR code. Everyone picks a card in secret. The host reveals the cards; the results show the average, the median and how far apart the votes are. If they are far apart, the people with the lowest and highest cards explain, and the team votes again.

Do I need an account?

No. You get a random username when you open the app, and you can change it. Usernames are deleted after 24 hours.

Is it free and open source?

Yes. It is free to use here and the code is GPL-3.0 on GitHub, so you can also host it yourself with Docker. Issues and pull requests are welcome.

What is the technology stack?

Angular with server-side rendering, .NET 9, GraphQL with a Fusion gateway, Redis and PostgreSQL.

What data is collected?

Ticket names and vote results are kept; usernames are deleted after 24 hours. No personal data is required to use the application.
