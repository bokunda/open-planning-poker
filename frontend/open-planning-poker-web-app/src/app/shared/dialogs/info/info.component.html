About Open Planning Poker

Open Planning Poker is a free, open-source planning poker app for agile teams. Create a room, share the link, vote in secret and turn the cards over together, so nobody anchors the team.

What you can do

  • Estimate tickets with Fibonacci, T-shirt sizes or your own deck, with a shared timer and auto-reveal.
  • Run the room with co-hosts, hand it over to someone else, and let people join as observers.
  • Pick the final estimate after the reveal, reorder and edit tickets, and export the results as PDF or CSV.
  • Vote before the meeting with async voting, and keep a team room with the sprint history and velocity.

About the creator

Open Planning Poker is built by Bojan Piskulic, founder of Bitcrafta, a software agency that designs and builds web applications for its clients. Bojan created Open Planning Poker as the estimation tool he wanted for his own teams: free, without sign-up or ads, and open source, so anyone can read the code or run it on their own server.

Privacy

The code is open, so you can check what is stored. Users are deleted 24 hours after they are created. Games, tickets and votes are kept longer, for team rooms, reports and statistics.
